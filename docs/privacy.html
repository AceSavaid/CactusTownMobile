Privacy Policy for Cactus Town

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Cactus Town ("the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and published by Ace Savaid.

Cactus Town does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no online features. Everything you do in the Game stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The Game does not ask for, collect, or transmit:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage/analytics data
  • Photos, contacts, files, or microphone or camera input
  • Payment information (the Game contains no purchases)

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally so you can continue where you left off. This includes information such as your coins, collected materials, plant customisation, town repair progress, mini-game unlocks, and audio settings. This saved data:

  • Is stored only in the Game's private storage on your device
  • Is never uploaded or transmitted, and is not accessible to the developer or anyone else
  • Is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data from Android settings

Permissions

The Game requests no special Android permissions and has no internet access.

Third parties

The Game contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or social media software. It makes no network connections of its own.

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google's handling of the store itself is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Because the Game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It is suitable for a general audience.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, adding an online feature — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above revised. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the revised policy.
