## Full description (max 4000)

Cactus Town is a laid-back game about a little potted cactus with a big
to-do list. Grow your plant, tidy up a run-down town one bench and fountain at a
time, and unwind with a pocketful of mini-games — all offline, with no ads and
no in-app purchases.

**Look after your plant**
Pick your pot, your species, and an accessory or two — a top hat, shades, a bow
tie. However you dress your cactus up at home, that's how it looks everywhere
you go.

**Fix up the town**
Wander a cosy top-down town split into six neighbourhoods — a main square, a
garden, a park, a shopping street and more. The townsfolk (all cacti, of course)
point you toward worn-out fountains, benches, lamps and statues that need a
hand. Bring the right materials, make the repair, earn some coins. Restore a
whole neighbourhood and you can restyle everything in it — and the next area
opens up.

**Gather materials**
Head out to four regions — Forest, Flower Field, River and Cave — and collect
wood, water, stone, flowers and ore through quick little gathering games.

**Play mini-games**
The house arcade has seven: Tic-Tac-Toe, Pong, Match the Pairs, Minesweeper,
Snake, Sudoku and Blackjack. Pick a difficulty, beat it, pocket the coins.

**Made to be gentle**
• Plays in landscape, easy one-handed
• Fully offline — your progress stays on your device
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no sign-in
• The town shifts a little each day, so there's always a small thing to do
